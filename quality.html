Product Testing

Our Testing Process

Burst Pressure Testing

Hoses and assemblies are subjected to pressure beyond rated limits to confirm structural integrity and safety margin before release to client.

Salt Spray Testing

Corrosion resistance of metal tubes, fittings, and coated surfaces verified through standardised salt spray exposure cycles per DIN and ISO protocols.

Impulse Testing

Cyclical pressure impulse testing simulates real-world pressure fluctuations and validates long-term durability of hose assemblies under dynamic conditions.

Leak Testing

Pneumatic and hydraulic leak tests on all hose assemblies and tube connections before packing approval. Zero tolerance for leaks at dispatch.

Dimensional Inspection

Critical dimensions verified using calibrated tools and 3D scanning equipment for geometric accuracy and OEM fitment compliance.

Pull Test & Crimp Verification

Pull-out force and crimping height verified on every hose assembly to ensure fitting integrity under rated and over-rated load conditions.
