Who We Are

Overview

Specialized in manufacturing automotive brake hoses, fuel pipes, hydraulic hoses, and industrial piping systems with high quality standards and traceability systems.

As a leading manufacturer of high-performance hydraulic hoses, pipe and fitting solutions, as well as other industrial products based in Egypt, we proudly serve clients across Egypt, the Middle East and beyond.

We supply a variety of industries including Automotive, Aerospace, Oil & Gas, Agriculture, Air Cooling, Fire Fighting, Pharmaceutical, Construction, Railway, Water & Waste Management, and Energy & Utilities, amongst others.

At the heart of our operations is a commitment to providing reliable, durable, and efficient products that address unique operational challenges and drive performance.

Innovation lies at the core of our success. Through dedicated Research and Development (R&D), we continuously enhance materials, processes, and product functionality to meet evolving industry requirements while minimizing environmental impact. Our sustainable practices include eco-friendly manufacturing, responsible sourcing, and solutions aligned with global environmental standards.

Collaboration is central to our approach. We work closely with our clients to understand their specific needs and challenges. We aspire to become their reliable supply partner.

We adhere to the highest industry standards, ensuring that every product meets best-practice manufacturing processes and delivers to exact specification requirements. Through a commitment to customer satisfaction, continuous product improvement, rigorous testing, innovative practices, and sustainability initiatives, we provide solutions that build trust and foster long-term partnerships.

Quality Assurance

Our Commitment to Quality

QA forms the cornerstone of our production process.

Procuring standardised raw materials, state-of-the-art machinery and equipment, experienced and certified engineers and workers, rigorous testing, and controlled packaging and delivery are the foundations of our commitment to quality. To meet the specifications of our clients and maintain best practice processes, we focus on Research and Development — at times in coordination with our clients — to ensure that the final product is uniquely designed and manufactured to meet required specifications. Traceability of non-compliant items is an ongoing process to ensure constant product quality and continuous realignment and calibration of processes and machinery.

🔬 Raw Material Standards

Every incoming batch from approved suppliers is inspected and logged. If reject rate exceeds 2%, the next batch undergoes 100% inspection.

⚙️ Manufacturing Controls

Detailed drawings per item, dimension checks after cutting, geometry checks after bending, coating thickness tests after electroplating — every step documented.

🧪 Rigorous Testing

Hose assembly, crimping, pull tests, crimping height verification, cleanliness validation, and leak and pressure testing on every batch before dispatch.

✅ Final QC Sign-Off

QC Inspector approval, final inspection, batch log entry, and packing approval — nothing ships without a complete quality sign-off.

📦 Batch Traceability

Every product is barcoded with full batch traceability entry before entering the finished goods warehouse, ensuring end-to-end accountability.

🏆 IATF 16949:2016 & ISO 9001:2015 — Integrated

Internationally recognised automotive quality management standard with an integrated ISO 9001:2015 system. Certificate No. TS/1209/25, valid through October 2028, issued by RINA/CISQ Automotive.

Co-Founder & Chairman
